Decisions

Rules this layout follows

Placement

  • The stage is the layout. Chrome floats over it, never beside it.
  • Output actions live in the title bar, because they finish the document rather than edit it.
  • Editing controls live in one bar, grouped by job.
  • Low-frequency choices hide behind a control and open in a popover.
  • A mode that changes the image (backdrop, colour) shows itself on the image.

Still open

  • Should the backdrop control also offer a plain "off" row in the grid, or is the toggle enough?
  • Does the colour chip need RGB beside the hex?
  • Should the bar auto-hide while drawing, or after a few idle seconds?
  • Does the tight window drop the backdrop label before dropping tools?
